# Facilitator guide

## Session plan

A 60-minute core session with a further 30 minutes reserved for questions, access needs or technical delays. The confirmed calendar block is October 2, 2026 18:00–19:30 CEST / 11:00–12:30 America/Chicago.

| Minute | Activity |
| --- | --- |
| 0–5 | Welcome, speaker introduction, learning goals |
| 5–13 | Everyday AI risks, evidence and uncertainty |
| 13–20 | Data minimization, source instructions and unsupported assumptions |
| 20–27 | Demonstrate two claims in Sample A and the review worksheet |
| 27–47 | Run the 20-minute lab |
| 47–55 | Debrief with answer key and corrected announcement |
| 55–60 | Questions and a reusable review checklist |
| 60–90 | Optional questions, additional practice and contingency buffer |

## Demo rehearsal

Open README, SOURCE-PACK, SAMPLE-OUTPUTS and WORKSHEET in separate browser tabs. Show F2 next to the draft's date. Then show F7 next to the recording promise. Explain the difference between a contradicted fact and an unsupported guarantee. Avoid revealing every answer before participants work. For the debrief, open ANSWER-KEY.

No live model is needed. If an optional tool fails, continue with the supplied sample outputs. Test the repository links before the session. Increase browser zoom for readability, describe evidence aloud, and allow paper or plain text responses. Do not require participants to disclose disabilities or private records.

## Discussion prompts

- Which supported detail could you accidentally delete while correcting the draft?
- Why is the recording claim unknown rather than a proven falsehood?
- Who should approve the registration link?
- What changes when an assistant can send messages or alter records?
- Which task from your own work would benefit from this review record?

## Scope

Examples illustrate general review practices. They do not establish compliance with any law or replace an organization's approved process. Keep discussion of real employment, medical or other consequential decisions at the level of human accountability; do not evaluate individual people's eligibility during this workshop.

## Cleanup

No cloud resources, keys or payments are used. Close optional model sessions and remove local fictional work if no longer needed. Do not collect participant worksheets containing personal details.
